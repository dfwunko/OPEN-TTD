💡 Getting Started Tips
  1. Locate a primary producer (such as a Coal Mine or Forest) near a power plant or sawmill.
  2. Build train stations with two platforms next to both locations and connect them with railway tracks.
  3. Build a train depot, buy a steam or diesel engine, and attach freight wagons.
  4. Add orders for the train: "Load full cargo" at the mine, and "Unload" at the power plant.
  5. Press the red flag on the train window to set it to green and start earning cash!
