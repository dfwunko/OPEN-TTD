How to Play

Pick one of three Bindings, then bind up to four veteran cards out of your Codex - cards you have already fought with, carrying every upgrade and every scar they have earned. Descend fifteen floors. Each turn you draw five cards and spend three Focus: tap a card to lift it, tap an enemy to strike. Guard vanishes at the start of your next turn, so spend it. Cards of the same faction chain together when you play them back to back, and chains score - though the later links of a fight score less than the first ones. Between fights you draft one card from three, buy Seals at the Scriptorium, and choose your path. Fell three bosses to clear the run. Whatever happens, the cards in your deck come home changed: every card that helped fell a boss earns a permanent upgrade, and when you die, the Blot writes one permanent scar on a card you choose.
